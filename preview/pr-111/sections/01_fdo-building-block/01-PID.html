Persistent Identifiers

Persistent Identifier are a critical FDO building block without which FDOs cannot exist. This document acknowledges that there can be many different sorts of PIDs and PID systems and this specification has only the most general requirements and makes a set of recommendations.

[R-PID-1] PID Properties

An FDO PID MUST be a globally unique, persistent, resolvable and manageable identifier.

[R-PID-2] PID Resolution

An FDO PID MUST resolve into a machine readable PID record using well-defined access protocols with open specifications. This PID record MAY contain or refer to an FDO record.

[R-PID-3] PID Persistence

An FDO’s PID MUST persist for a least 10 years after the resource it identifies is no longer available to insure referential stability.

[R-PID-3] PID Assignment

An FDO’s PID MUST never re-assigned to identify any other resource.

Recommendations

PIDs
A PID should avoid semantic relevance unless absolutely necessary.
Administration Persistence
A PID's persistence depends on a PID’s administrator’s promise and ability to manage the PID over time. PID administrators should have clear, public policies and procedure describing how they will persist their PIDs.
PID Service Provider
A PID’s persistence depends directly on the PID service provider’s ability to consistently operate its PID service.
  • A PID service provider should be an independent, neutral, non-profit, multi-stake holder organization with global governance that can provide its PID service equally around the world for long periods.
  • A PID service provider should maintain it up to date with the specifications that it implements.

ID creators should ensure that they their choice of PID service providers are appropriate for their use.

PID Service
A PID service is a system that stores and manages PIDs and their PID records.
  • A PID service should be globally distributed to ensure consistent PID resolution and management around the globe. It should facilitate digital sovereignty
  • A PID service should have high resolution and management performance and ability to scale.
  • A PID service should offer a high degree of security to ensure that only designated entities can manage its PIDs and their associated PID records.
  • A PID service should provide options to restrict access to the whole record or to individual attributes.
  • APID service SHOULD provide solutions for ensuring the integrity, availability, and authenticity of the PID record.
