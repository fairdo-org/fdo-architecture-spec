Validation

This section is normative

Validation is a key characteristic of FDOs. It enables detecting errors in the FDO record early on. Validation SHOULD at least be carried out upon creation and update of an FDO.

[R-VAL-1] Profile Validation

Every FDO record instance MUST at least be valid against 0.FDO/Root. It furthermore MUST be valid against all other profiles it claims to instantiate. Being valid against a profile means that the record MUST contain all attributes that are required by the profile with the required cardinalities.

[R-VAL-2] Attribute Validation

All attributes in the FDO record that instantiate attribute definitions MUST be valid against the validation rules imposed by the attribute definitions, see also .
