FDO Data Validation

While formal validation of the data bit-sequence(s) against the FDO type or associated attributes is recognized as a critical requirement in certain application contexts, this specification does not currently define a mechanism for expressing or enforcing such validation rules. As a result, the decision to perform data validation, along with the selection of applicable validation criteria, remains optional and implementation-dependent. The extent and nature of validation practices across communities are expected to vary. Capturing these practices will inform the development of a standardized validation framework in future versions of this specification, such that data validation could become a generic processing step in the future.
