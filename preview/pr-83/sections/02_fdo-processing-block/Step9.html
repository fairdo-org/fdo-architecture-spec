[S-9]: FDO Metadata Inspection

In this step, the content of the FDO record and potential associated metadata files are inspected to determine whether they describe a resource that may be of interest to the accessing client. The specific inspection procedure and the evaluation criteria applied are determined by the client.

Clients may have different requirements such as requiring fully valid FDOs with valid FDO profiles and verified attributes, while others may have less restrictive needs. Also, it could be that a client requires that certain attributes exist or have a specific value, or that the content of referenced metadata files is as desired.

Inputs
  • an FDO record: This step expects exactly one well-structured FDO record. Typically, this is the output of .
Outputs
The output is a status:
  • true: The FDO is of interest to the client and he may proceed to the next step.
  • false: The FDO is not of any interest to the client.
Expected behavior
The client may use all the attributes and potential metadata files (that he finds by inspecting the 0.FDO/Metadata attribute and following external references) to come to a conclusion, based on his internal evaluation criteria.
