[S-9]: FDO Metadata Inspection

This step assumes that was completed successfully.

The content of the FDO record and potential associated metadata files are inspected to determine whether they describe a resource that may be of interest to the accessing client. The specific inspection procedure and the evaluation criteria applied are determined by the client.

Users may impose additional restrictions depending on the information they are looking for. For example, users may require that certain attributes exist or have a specific value, or that the content of referenced metadata files is as desired. Hence, this may result in users implementing this function themselves.

Inputs
  • a list of validated_attribute as outputted by
Outputs
The output is a status:
  • true: The FDO is of interest to the client, and he may proceed to the next step.
  • false: The FDO is not of any interest to the client.
Expected behavior
The client may use all the attributes and potential metadata files (that he finds by inspecting the 0.FDO/Metadata attribute and following external references) to come to a conclusion, based on his internal evaluation criteria.
