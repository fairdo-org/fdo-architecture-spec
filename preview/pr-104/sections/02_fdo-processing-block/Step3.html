[S-3]: Parsing the PID Record into FDO Attributes

This step assumes that successfully returned at least one PID record, which it converts into a set of FDO attributes.
Inputs
  • a PID record: This step expects exactly one syntactically valid PID record in the format of choosing of the identifier technology. Typically, this is the output of .
Outputs
The result of this step is an FDO record with all its individual FDO attributes parsed. At this point, the information inside the record and its attributes is not yet validated against the attribute definitions and profiles. The output is an object that consists out of the following information:
  • pid: The PID the record was resolved with.
  • attributes: A list of FDO attributes in form of tuples.
Expected behavior
This step is expected to transform a valid PID record into a valid FDO record. The concrete realization is specific to every FDO implementation. This step must terminate within a reasonable timeframe (e.g., of < 10 ms). It is not intended to perform network requests, or if it does, it caches the information for efficiency purposes.

This step only extracts FDO attributes out of the PID record and discards the remainder of the PID record. If needed, some PID technology-specific information MAY be transformed into FDO attributes. Any other information extracted from the PID record such as intrinsic metadata are not requirements of the specification but will be described within the respective implementation profile. Examples are time stamps of resolution in [[!RFC3339]] format, a time to live in seconds, and possibly other implementation-specific fields.
